# Research — high-quality garment masks + seam-free workflow assembly (2026-06-25)

Trigger: owner — "потрібні більш якісні маски і граматно зібраний воркфлоу; всі фінали виглядають
коряво, наче сшиті з різних шматків." Goal: find genuinely high-quality, *locally deployable* options
(not only what is already installed) for (A) garment masks and (B) a seam-free assembly, applicable to our
case (QIE base on the person → repair specific garments → coherent final, RTX 4090 16 GB, ComfyUI).

## 0. Why the current finals look "stitched" — confirmed by the literature, not our assembly bug
Independent sources confirm the patchwork look is an **architectural property** of per-garment-FitDiT + hard
composite, not a wiring mistake:
- FitDiT "achieves texture preservation **at the cost of distorting the person's actual body shape**" — matches
  our measured proportion drift (shoulder/hip ratio 3.6 %→7.2 % across the two passes). [DS-VTON / survey]
- FitDiT "produces **noticeably brighter garments**" (tonal inconsistency); Leffa darker — matches our tonal
  seams at the composite edges. [survey]
- Diffusion compositing of independently generated segments is "**less harmonized and less well-blended at
  segment edges**" — the patchwork itself. [Composite Diffusion]
Conclusion: two independent FitDiT generations (each with its own tone + its own body model) pasted through
coarse masks **cannot** be made seamless by mask tuning alone. The fix is architectural.

## A. High-quality garment masks (deployable locally)

### A1. ComfyUI-RMBG (1038lab) — single install, covers most of our needs  ★ recommended entry point
One package provides, with MASK **and** IMAGE outputs:
- **Clothes Segment** — SegFormer fine-tuned on **ATR, 18 semantic classes** incl. upper-clothes, skirt, dress,
  pants, with **multi-item selection**. Semantic ⇒ upper-clothes ≠ skirt by construction → **directly kills the
  "blouse mask eats the skirt" failure** (that was grounded_sam's connectivity blob merging blouse+skirt).
- **Fashion Segment** — segformer-b3-fashion, finer fashion taxonomy.
- **BiRefNet** suite (general/portrait/matting/**HR up to 2560²**) + **SDMatte** — alpha-matte edges
  (soft boundaries) instead of hard binary cut → seam-free composite edges.
- SAM / SAM2 / **SAM3** + GroundingDINO (text-prompted), better edges than our current GroundingDINO+SAM-HQ.
Models are small/medium (SegFormer ~0.1–0.3 GB, BiRefNet ~1 GB) → trivial alongside QIE on 16 GB.

### A2. Meta Sapiens (ComfyUI_Sapiens / comfyui-sapiens2) — quality ceiling
**28-class body-part segmentation, natively 1K (1024²)**, ViT-MAE pretrained on human images; Seg-1B fits 16 GB
(2B is tighter when co-loaded with QIE). Highest-quality human/garment-region masks available. Use if SegFormer
edges are not clean enough on a given garment.

### A3. SCHP / ATR-18 (we already have ATR in Python via Leffa)
Classic self-correcting human parser; same ATR taxonomy as A1. Already proven in our board builder. Lower edge
quality than SegFormer-clothes+matte or Sapiens, but zero new install.

**Mask recommendation:** semantic source = **SegFormer-Clothes (ATR-18)** (separates garments, no bleed) →
refine edge with **BiRefNet/SDMatte alpha matte** (soft boundary). Escalate to **Sapiens Seg-1B** only where
SegFormer is visibly wrong. This replaces both the grounded_sam blobs (variant A/B) and the FitDiT rectangles
(variant F).

## B. Seam-free assembly (anti-stitch), deployable in ComfyUI

### B1. Differential Diffusion (native ComfyUI node) + Gaussian Blur Mask + InpaintModelConditioning  ★
"Soft inpainting": a **gradient** (not binary) mask drives **per-pixel** denoise strength → smooth transition
between kept and regenerated pixels, no hard seam. Standard 3-node recipe. Crucially it works with a normal
checkpoint — **so we can run it through QIE itself**, meaning the repaired region is generated by the **same
model at the same tone** as the base → removes FitDiT's brighter-garment tonal mismatch at the root.

### B2. Final harmonization pass — one low-denoise full-image refine
After any composite, a single **QIE img2img at denoise ≈ 0.2–0.3 over the whole frame** unifies tone/lighting/
sharpness and melts residual seams (classic "paste → harmonize via denoising"). Cheap, model already loaded.

### B3. Soft-matte edges instead of GrowMask+Feather
Replace the hard grow/erode/feather chain with an **alpha matte** (SDMatte/BiRefNet) so the composite boundary is
inherently soft and follows the true garment edge.

### B4. Research-grade (paper-only, no turnkey node yet — context, not for now)
HarmonPaint (training-free soft-attention harmonization), Blend-Aware Latent Diffusion (blend-aware VAE +
harmonizing loss), diffusion image-harmonization. Deployable equivalents today = B1+B2.

## C. Architectural direction — the actual answer to "граматно зібраний воркфлоу"
The field is moving to **mask-free / single coherent generation** (OmniTry "try-on anything without masks",
JCo-MVTON, DS-VTON) *because* per-region compositing stitches. Our **QIE base is already a single coherent
generation** (seam-free, garment-correct ~80 % of seeds). The stitching is introduced **only** by the FitDiT
composite passes — which the literature independently flags for body-shape distortion + tonal shift.

Therefore the well-assembled pipeline **inverts the current design**:
1. **Primary = holistic QIE base** (seam-free by construction).
2. **Gate** each garment (sim + colour). If all pass → ship the base, no repair, no seam.
3. **Repair only the failing garment**, and do it as **differential-diffusion soft inpainting inside QIE**
   (same model/tone) using a **semantic clothes mask (SegFormer/SCHP) with a soft matte edge** — not a second
   foreign model (FitDiT) with its own tone and body geometry.
4. **Finish = one low-denoise harmonization pass** to unify.
5. **Drop FitDiT from the default path** (it is the source of both the proportion distortion and the tonal seam);
   keep it only as an experimental alternative for shape-repair cases where QIE-inpaint can't fix silhouette.

This keeps one model, one tone, semantic clean masks, soft edges, and a final unify — i.e. removes every cause
of the patchwork look identified in §0.

## D. Applicability / cost on 16 GB
- SegFormer-clothes, BiRefNet, SDMatte: small, trivial.
- Sapiens Seg-1B: fits; 2B tight when co-loaded with QIE.
- Differential Diffusion + harmonization: **no new model** (reuse QIE) → near-zero extra VRAM, only time.
- Net: the recommended path is *lighter* than the current QIE+2×FitDiT+SAM+GDINO stack, which co-loads 3 heavy
  models and pressured VRAM (a suspected contributor to the base non-determinism observed 2026-06-25).

## E. ADDENDUM (2026-06-25, later) — freshest 2025–2026 work I under-weighted in the first pass
First pass anchored on models I knew from training (SegFormer 2022, SCHP 2019, Sapiens-1 2024, FitDiT,
Differential Diffusion). The live search *did* return newer hits; pursuing them changes two things:

### E1. Masks — the current SOTA is Sapiens2, not Sapiens-1/SegFormer
- **Sapiens2 (Meta, released 2026-04-24; facebookresearch/sapiens2)** — 1K ViT pretrained on **1B** human
  images; body-part seg + **surface normals** + pointmaps + albedo. "Pixel-level masks delineating the body and
  **specific clothing items**", pixel-accurate hair, sharper **rare classes (earrings, lips)**; dense-probing
  beats DINOv3-7B. ComfyUI wrapper exists (lassiiter/comfyui-sapiens2). → **New top pick for mask quality**, and
  its **normals** double as a body-shape signal (see E2). SegFormer-Clothes stays as the light fallback.

### E2. The body-shape distortion ("stitched/bulkier") now has a dedicated 2025–2026 line
This is the real cause of our patchwork, and the field addressed it directly (mostly research-stage code):
- **FitControler (2025-12, arXiv 2512.24016)** — a **learnable plug-in that drops into existing VTON models**
  via the **ControlNet interface** to enforce fit: garment-agnostic preprocessor from **keypoints + body
  proportions**, a fit-aware layout generator, a multi-scale fit injector. Purpose-built for the exact "VTON
  neglects fit / distorts body" gap.
- **FIT dataset + method (2026-04, arXiv 2604.08526)** — "near-perfect identity and body-shape preservation by
  explicitly conditioning on the **identity map and ground-truth normals**." Transferable insight: **condition on
  body normals to preserve shape** — and Sapiens2 produces those normals locally.
- **OmniVTON++ (2026-02, arXiv 2602.14552)** — **training-free**, "Structured Garment Morphing" for anatomically
  consistent adaptation + "Principal Pose Guidance" to preserve human structure; SOTA cross-dataset. Training-free
  ⇒ most deployable of the new try-on methods.
- **FitVTON** — fit-aware via structured text size prompts + parameterized-garment triplets.

### E3. Mask-free is now an explicit, well-supported paradigm (strengthens our "invert" plan)
- **MFP-VTON (2025-02, arXiv 2502.01626)** and **JCo-MVTON (2025-08, arXiv 2508.17614)** — mask-free DiT try-on;
  no mask ⇒ original details preserved ⇒ no composite seam. Independent support for §C: a single coherent
  generation (our QIE base) is the seam-free substrate; avoid per-region masked composite where possible.

### E4. Net effect on the recommendation
- §C (invert to QIE-centric, soft-inpaint, harmonize, drop FitDiT from default) is **unchanged in direction and
  now better supported** by the 2026 mask-free + fit-aware literature.
- **Mask source upgraded: lead with Sapiens2** (quality + normals), SegFormer-Clothes as the light option.
- **New lever for shape preservation:** feed **Sapiens2 normals / keypoints** as a structure condition (the
  FIT/FitControler insight) so any repair keeps the base body shape — addresses distortion at the source.
- FitControler / OmniVTON++ / FitVTON are **research-stage** (verify code/license before counting on them); treat
  as direction, not turnkey. Sapiens2 + Differential-Diffusion + harmonization are deployable now.
