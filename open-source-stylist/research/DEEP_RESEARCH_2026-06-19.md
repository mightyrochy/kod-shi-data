## 1. How diffusion VTON actually works (authoritative)

### 1.1 The inpainting formulation
Modern image-based VTON is **latent diffusion inpainting** conditioned on a garment. The person image
is turned into a **garment-agnostic** image: the region that will hold the new garment is removed
(masked / zeroed), and the model regenerates only that region, conditioned on (a) a **pose/structure
prior** and (b) the **garment appearance**. Everything outside the mask is the original image.

Concretely, in **Leffa** (read from `leffa/model.py` + `leffa/pipeline.py`), the generative UNet's input
is **12 channels**, concatenated:
`noisy_latent(4) + mask(1) + masked_image_latent(4) + densepose(3)`.
- `masked_image = src_image * (mask < 0.5)` — the agnostic region is **zeroed** (`pipeline.py:64`).
- **DensePose is a hard input channel**, not attention. It is a per-pixel IUV body-part map. Being
  concatenated at the input, it is a **strong structural prior** that fixes the body/pose/limb layout
  the model must fill. This is the single most important fact for our failures (§2).
- The garment is injected **not** through this channel and **not** through text (cross-attention is
  *removed* — `remove_cross_attention`, so `encoder_hidden_states=None`). It enters through a second
  network.

### 1.2 How the garment is injected — reference UNet vs single UNet
Two dominant designs:
- **Dual-UNet (Leffa, IDM-VTON):** a second "reference" UNet encodes the garment image into multi-scale
  features that are concatenated into the **self-attention** keys/values of the generative UNet. The
  trained attention learns garment→body correspondence. Leffa's contribution ("flow field in
  attention") is a **training-time loss** that supervises the attention map so target queries attend to
  the correct garment keys — it sharpens detail; it is not an inference-time module.
- **Single-UNet (CatVTON, Re-CatVTON):** the garment latent is simply **concatenated in the spatial
  dimension** with the person latent and the one UNet attends across both. Much cheaper.

**Authoritative limitation of the dual-UNet reference design** (Re-CatVTON, arXiv 2511.18775): a
"**functional mismatch**" — the reference UNet was pretrained to expect *noisy* inputs but is fed *clean*
garment latents, so its garment features are strong at early denoising steps and "**collapse into noise
near t=1**". OOTDiffusion's fix (fixed-timestep reference) makes features static and timestep-insensitive,
which is also suboptimal because diffusion recovers different frequencies at different timesteps. This is
the mechanistic reason garment fidelity in Leffa/IDM-VTON is imperfect — and it is a property of the
architecture, not of our inputs. Re-CatVTON (full UNet fine-tune + timestep-aligned features + loss
exclusion on the garment region + improved CFG that removes the garment from the unconditional branch)
beats Leffa on FID (4.438 vs 4.540) and KID (0.010 vs 0.050) at ~half the compute and **2.26 GB / 1.3 s**.

### 1.3 The agnostic mask is decisive — and category-specific
The preprocessing (agnostic mask + densepose + pose keypoints) is generated by a **human parser
(SCHP, trained on LIP/ATR)** + **DensePose** + **OpenPose**. The agnostic mask is *not* "the garment
silhouette" — it is a learned region the model was trained to fill.

From the actual DressCode code (`leffa_utils/utils.py::get_agnostic_mask_dc`):
- **lower_body** agnostic = `pants(6) + left-leg(12) + right-leg(13)`, dilated; upper-clothes + arms are
  *fixed* (preserved). So for a lower garment the model is trained to **regenerate the legs together with
  the garment**, using densepose for leg structure. The legs are *inside* the agnostic by design.
- **upper_body** = `upper-clothes(4)`; skirt/pants fixed.
- **dresses** = upper + lower + legs.

**Authoritative rule** (survey, arXiv 2503.20418 and the field generally): *"Incorrect agnostic masks
prompt the model to hallucinate garments to fit the mask; a correct garment mask produces satisfactory
results."* The agnostic must match the training distribution of masks. A hand-drawn mask that does not
look like an SCHP-parser agnostic is out-of-distribution and the model improvises.

### 1.4 What VTON is bad at (documented, field-wide)
- **Coordinated multi-garment (upper+lower together):** most methods are single-garment; "recent systems
  struggle with the inability to generate coordinated upper and lower garments" (survey 2503.20418).
- **Identity-specific attributes & exact body shape:** "existing studies often fail to preserve
  identity-specific attributes like tattoos, accessories, scars, or detailed body shape" (ibid).
- **In-the-wild / non-studio:** the datasets (VITON-HD, DressCode) are clean frontal studio shots.
- **Accessories** (belts, jewellery, shoes): out of scope for classic garment VTON.

## 2. Why our 2026-06-18 problems happened (grounded in §1)

These are now explained by §1, replacing the chain of image-derived theories I committed (reclassified
as Observations in RECONCILIATION_2026-06-19.md).

- **Skirt rendered as trousers; "skirt melts into legs".** Two compounding causes, both in §1.1/1.3:
  (a) **densepose is a hard channel** dictating two-leg structure; the garment (attention) is the weaker
  signal; (b) **my hand-built masks were out-of-distribution agnostics**, so the model hallucinated to
  fit them ("incorrect agnostic → hallucination", §1.3). When the mask covered the lower legs, the model
  was asked to regenerate legs+garment there and produced a skirt↔leg morph. None of this required a new
  theory — it is the documented behaviour of densepose-conditioned inpainting VTON with a bad agnostic.
- **Garment colour not transferring (brown→gray/black).** The dual-UNet **functional mismatch** (§1.2):
  garment features weaken through denoising, so with a low-signal garment image the output drifts to the
  context/prior colour. Re-CatVTON documents exactly this fidelity weakness of the Leffa-class design.
- **Contamination (grey sweater bleeding through the blouse).** Trivially explained: outside the mask the
  original is preserved verbatim (`masked_image = src*(mask<0.5)`); the agnostic mask was too small, so
  the old collar/waist survived. Not a model defect — a mask-coverage issue.
- **Bare-legs effect.** A person in opaque full-length trousers has the lower body parsed as `pants` with
  **no leg labels (12/13)**, so the lower-body agnostic is pants-shaped and densepose still says "legs":
  the model reconstructs trousers/legs. Exposing the legs (FLUX-Fill jeans→bare legs) put the input back
  into the DressCode distribution (bare legs + a short lower garment), which the model handles. This is a
  *real, source-consistent* observation, but it is a **preprocessing workaround for using a
  studio-trained garment VTON on an in-the-wild person** — it is not a property the product should rely
  on. It belongs in the "if we use dedicated VTON" branch only.

**Bottom line for §2:** every failure is a known, documented behaviour of studio-trained,
densepose-conditioned, dual-UNet garment VTON applied (a) to an in-the-wild full-body person, (b) with
hand-built out-of-distribution masks, (c) for a long lower-body garment, (d) under the dual-UNet fidelity
ceiling. We were fighting the tool's design, off-distribution, with the wrong masks.

## 3. Two families, and why the design chose editing-models-first

### 3.1 Dedicated garment VTON (IDM-VTON, CatVTON/Re-CatVTON, OOTDiffusion, Leffa, FitDiT, FastFit, Voost)
- **Strengths:** strong pose/structure adherence (explicit densepose/agnostic), good single-garment
  fidelity, efficient (CatVTON-class ~2–3 GB).
- **Weaknesses (all documented):** single-garment (no coordinated outfit), require a *correct* SCHP/
  DensePose agnostic, studio-distribution-bound, dual-UNet fidelity ceiling, weak on accessories, and the
  research consensus (Garments2Look user study) that they **lose on layering**. Many are NC-licensed
  (IDM-VTON, CatVTON, FitDiT, AnyDressing) — prototype-only.

### 3.2 General editing models (Qwen-Image-Edit-2511, FLUX Kontext, Gemini "Nano Banana")
- **Strengths:** instruction-following, native multi-image conditioning, handle **in-the-wild** photos and
  **layering** by parsing the request, no agnostic-mask requirement, one model for the whole outfit.
- **Weaknesses:** weaker structural/pose control and lower garment-detail fidelity than a dedicated VTON
  ("Nano Banana lacks inpainting and explicit pose control → underperforms specialized VTON on fidelity
  and structural consistency", OpenVTON-Bench 2601.22725); known **colour-shift** on clothing edits for
  the Qwen-Image-Edit class (design §3.7).

### 3.3 The design's thesis (already correct — I diverged from it on 2026-06-18)
`SYSTEM_DESIGN §3` (verified against primary sources) already establishes:
- **item 2:** general editing models **beat** dedicated VTON on **layered outfits** (Garments2Look user
  study: FastFit, a leading multi-ref VTON, scored *lowest* on layering).
- **item 4:** industrial SOTA (Tstars-Tryon 1.0, Alibaba/Taobao) converged on a **multi-reference
  editing-model** architecture with engineered identity + background control.
- **item 1/3:** outfit-level try-on is an *open research problem*; control degrades as simultaneous
  reference count grows → group/sequence references.
- Hence the design's default: **Strategy A — holistic QIE-2511 edit + restoration shell + per-region
  gates**, with **Strategy C — holistic clothing pass + per-item accessory passes** as the measured
  alternative; FLUX Fill/OmniTry only for targeted repair/accessories. The whole E-010 detour into
  FitDiT/Leffa was a step *away* from this and was never reconciled — that is the core process error to
  correct.

**The synthesis:** the product is an **outfit** on a **real in-the-wild person** with **identity + body
preservation**. That is the editing-model regime, not the single-garment studio-VTON regime. Dedicated
VTON, warping, and OmniTry are **supporting tools for specific sub-problems**, not the spine.

## 5. Identity, body, pose, background — the preservation stack

VTON/editing models do not preserve these for free; the design's **P1 (minimise trusted-generation
surface)** is the right frame.
- **Identity (face):** ArcFace cosine gate (project has it, V-ID-001, thr 0.57). Editing models keep
  identity better than re-synthesis VTON when conditioned on the person image; for repair passes,
  protect-by-construction (mask excludes the face). Cloud/industrial systems add explicit identity
  control (Tstars-Tryon; PuLID/InstantID in open stacks).
- **Body shape:** documented weak point ("fails to preserve detailed body shape"). Measured by SMPL
  registration + Chamfer distance in research; our proxy is the proportions gate (body-bbox, framing-
  caveated). DensePose/SMPL-shape control is the lever; the QIE ~9 % hip-slim is the symptom.
- **Pose:** densepose/OpenPose conditioning holds pose in VTON; editing models hold it loosely → a pose
  ControlNet is the lever if needed.
- **Background:** composite original back (design shell step 2) — deterministic, zero-risk.

## 6. Evaluation — what to measure, with what (validates the project's instruments)

Authoritative metric set (multiple 2025 sources, VTONQA 2601.02945, OpenVTON-Bench 2601.22725):
- **Realism / distribution:** FID (global; misses instance errors), KID.
- **Person/background preservation:** LPIPS / DreamSim on the non-garment region; SSIM/PSNR (pixel,
  penalise legitimate variation — weak).
- **Garment fidelity:** **DISTS** ("more reliable for garment image similarity"), LPIPS/DreamSim on the
  garment region, **CLIP-I / DINO (M-CLIP-I, M-DINO)** semantic similarity, and **retrieval rank vs decoys**
  (FashionCLIP / Fashion-SigLIP) for "is this the *specific* item".
- **Identity:** ArcFace cosine. **Body:** SMPL+D Chamfer.
- **Critical caveat (everyone agrees):** objective metrics **correlate weakly with human perception**;
  pixel scores penalise legitimate spatial variation; FID misses distorted textures / wrong patterns.
  → **human evaluation remains mandatory.**

**This validates the project's existing stack** (`system/gates/garment_fidelity.py`: FashionSigLIP
retrieval-rank + DISTS; ArcFace identity; owner's eye as final). The honest gap is the same the project
already records (§6b ledger): **no calibrated garment-fidelity threshold** (needs a labelled set), and
texture/silhouette/absolute-shade gaps. Nothing here contradicts the project; it confirms the direction.

## 7. Recommended architecture (reconciled with SYSTEM_DESIGN)

**Return to the design's editing-model-first spine. Treat dedicated VTON / warping / OmniTry as
optional sub-tools, not the engine.** Concretely for V1:

1. **Engine = QIE-2511 (Apache-2.0), holistic edit, multi-image conditioning** (Strategy A), with the
   community **try-on LoRA + multi-image ComfyUI workflow** as the adopt-first baseline (P8). It handles
   the in-the-wild full-body person + the *outfit* + layering in one model — exactly where dedicated VTON
   failed us. Known risk: colour-shift → solve deterministically in the shell (per-region LAB matching
   toward the reference), already designed.
2. **Strategy C fallback** (holistic clothing pass + **OmniTry** accessory passes for belt/earrings/shoes)
   if the holistic pass can't place accessories — OmniTry is the commercial-OK, mask-free accessory tool.
3. **Per-region gates + restoration shell** exactly as designed (identity ArcFace; colour CIEDE2000;
   proportions; DISTS+FashionSigLIP garment fidelity; background composite; conditional face restore).
4. **Dedicated VTON (Leffa MIT / FitDiT) only as a *single-garment high-fidelity repair* executor**, and
   only if an experiment shows it beats QIE+repair on a specific garment — and then **only with a correct
   SCHP/DensePose agnostic** (never hand-drawn masks) and **only on an in-distribution (bare-leg-capable)
   crop**. The 2026-06-18 work shows the cost of using it as the spine.
5. **Warping: dropped** as a spine (negative result stands as an Observation; pasted look, studio-bound).
6. **Cloud (Gemini/Imagen VTON, Kling, FASHN): the V3 quality ceiling**, and a legitimate fallback if no
   local stack clears the owner's bar — design already sanctions this.

**Why this is the right call:** the product is *outfit on a real person with identity+body preserved*;
that is the editing-model regime (sources §3); dedicated VTON is a studio single-garment tool we kept
fighting off-distribution. The single biggest correction is **stop hand-building agnostic masks** — if
any garment VTON is used, its masks must come from its own SCHP/DensePose pipeline (incorrect masks →
hallucination, §1.3).

## 11. Broader context — image-gen foundations & the fashion-AI stack (V2/V3)

Beyond VTON, the owner asked for the wider field. Concise, with the parts that bear on this project.

### 11.1 Image-generation foundations (why the engine choices look the way they do)
- **Architecture shift U-Net → DiT.** Text-to-image moved from U-Net latent diffusion (SD1.5/SDXL) to
  **Diffusion Transformers** trained with **flow matching** (FLUX, Qwen-Image, HiDream, Z-Image). Flow
  matching stabilises/accelerates training and is the backbone of the modern editing models we care
  about. (ICLR-2026 blog "U-Nets to DiTs"; Lumina/Flag-DiT.)
- **Instruction editing = (source, target, instruction) triplet training.** InstructPix2Pix → EmuEdit →
  UltraEdit → HiDream-E1; QIE-2511 and FLUX Kontext are this class. They edit *in image space following an
  instruction* without masks — which is exactly why they handle in-the-wild people and layering, and why
  they are the design's spine. The editing-model arena (24 M votes, 39 models, Mar 2026) shows the class
  is mature and competitive — a real, fast-moving option, not a research toy.
- **Identity & structure control levers (open-source):**
  - **IP-Adapter** — image-prompt adapter (inject a reference image's features via decoupled
    cross-attention). General appearance/identity conditioning.
  - **PuLID** — identity via insightface + Eva-CLIP face embedding, face masked out of background before
    CLIP; high ID fidelity with low disruption.
  - **InstantID** — IdentityNet = ControlNet + IP-Adapter; face embedding as cross-attn into a ControlNet.
  - **ControlNet** (pose/depth/densepose) — structural conditioning.
  These are the tools to bolt **identity + pose preservation** onto an editing-model spine if the holistic
  pass drifts (design P1; Tstars-Tryon does the industrial version of this).

### 11.2 Fashion-AI stack (maps to V2 retrieval & V3 wardrobe)
- **Retrieval embeddings — SOTA is fashion-specific.** **Marqo-FashionSigLIP** (ViT-B-16-SigLIP, trained
  with Generalised Contrastive Learning over category/style/colour/material/keywords) reports **+57 %
  recall@1 over FashionCLIP-2.0**. The project **already uses FashionSigLIP** for the garment-fidelity
  gate — this confirms it is the right encoder for both (a) the §6 garment-fidelity instrument and (b)
  V2 catalog retrieval (item description/image → nearest product), exactly as SYSTEM_DESIGN §10 plans.
  Pair with **Qdrant** (local) as designed. Benchmark: LookBench (arXiv 2601.14706).
- **Parsing / attribute / segmentation datasets & models** (for the §6a garment-isolation parser and any
  fine-tuning): **DeepFashion** (50 cat, 1000 attr, landmarks), **DeepFashion2** (491K, 13 cat, pose+seg+
  re-id), **DeepFashion-MultiModal**, **Fashionpedia** (expert ontology, fine-grained), **MMFashion**
  toolbox. **SCHP / human-parsing (LIP/ATR)** is the right tool for clothes-vs-body parsing (the design's
  §6a production plan; it is also what DressCode/Leffa use for agnostics — §1.3). This is the
  better-than-GroundingDINO parser the project already identified it needs.
- **Stylist reasoning (V2):** the styling decision (what outfit, why) is an LLM problem, not an image
  problem — local Qwen3-class with retrieval, escalate to a stronger LLM only if local judgment is weak
  (design §10). The AI-stylist *product* space (Lookastic, etc.) is mature on *recommendation*; the open
  gap that this project targets is **faithful generation of the chosen outfit on the real person** — which
  is precisely the VTON/editing problem of §1–§7.

### 11.3 One-line strategic reading
The field has split into (a) **general instruction-editing DiTs** (versatile, in-the-wild, the product
spine) and (b) **specialised studio VTON** (precise but narrow). Fashion retrieval/parsing has mature,
fashion-specific, open tools (FashionSigLIP, SCHP, DeepFashion/Fashionpedia) the project already names.
The project's design is aligned with where the field is; the work is execution + measurement, not a new
architecture search.

### 12.4 Two product truths that constrain the generation problem
- **The reference board is an information bottleneck.** 768×768 / 9 cells → each reference survives at
  **1.3–25 % of its pixels** (CONCEPT_REVIEW §5.1: skirt 1.78 %, belt 1.31 %, buttons lost). The fix is
  **role separation**: board = *outfit composition/layering evidence*; a **full-resolution per-item
  `GarmentEvidencePack`** = *item identity/detail evidence*, consumed by **single-item high-res local
  repair**. This is exactly where dedicated VTON / FitDiT (high-fidelity single garment) has a *real,
  scoped role* — not as the spine (§7) but as the **repair executor for one garment at full resolution**.

  for 8 items (CONCEPT_REVIEW §5.3). This is *why* per-item evaluation (retrieval-rank vs decoys, not
  mean colour) + per-item repair + a strict AND verdict are structural, not cosmetic — and why the
  garment-fidelity instrument calibration (the §6b gap) is on the critical path.

## 14. Why the VTON detour happened, and the consistent failure pattern (BUILD_PLAN + archive)

### 14.1 The Phase-0 engine gate is what pushed us to dedicated VTON — and it's in tension with the design
`BUILD_PLAN.md` **Phase 0 (2026-06-16)** requires the product engine to expose **three channels in one
local workflow**: (1) garment-image conditioning, (2) masked edit/inpaint, (3) **body/pose control**.
Result (`ENGINE_CAPABILITY_PHASE0_2026-06-16.md`): **no local candidate passes** — QIE-2511's
control/model-patch weights aren't visible (`ModelPatchLoader` 0 options); FLUX-Fill lacks ControlNet/
CLIPVision; BFL FLUX is paid API. **This is the documented reason E-010 went to FitDiT/Leffa** — those
bundle garment-encoder + parsing + pose, i.e. they *do* pass the 3-channel gate.

**The tension:** the gate's **"explicit body/pose control"** requirement biases the choice toward
**dedicated VTON** (explicit densepose/pose) and *against* the **editing-model** spine the design (§3, §7)
actually chose — editing models (QIE board + try-on LoRA) handle pose **implicitly** and have no
ControlNet channel, so they "fail" Phase 0 by construction. So Phase 0, as written, quietly steers away
from the design's own editing-first decision. That is the structural reason the project oscillated.

**Resolution (for the owner):** EITHER (a) **relax Phase-0's explicit-control clause for editing-model
engines** — body/pose is carried implicitly by the person image, *measured* by `body_pose.py`, and
protected by P1 (compose body/face pixels back where possible); the QIE board+LoRA path (`run_slice.py`)
can be tried today without a control channel. OR (b) **add a pose/depth ControlNet** to QIE/FLUX (the
Family-5 assembly; cheap depth-conditioning from FIELD_SURVEY §5.3) so the editing spine *also* passes the
gate. Path (a) is the immediate, lowest-cost move; (b) is the body-shape upgrade if (a)'s body drift is
rejected. Either way the gate must stop disqualifying the design's own spine.

### 14.2 The failure pattern is constant since the first run (archive) — and the body mechanism is understood
`archive/FINDINGS.md` (2026-06-10, run_000001, QIE-2511+Lightning): identity **PASS**; the failures were
**accessories (belt/earrings), footwear (colour+type), skirt length, body slim, blouse colour** — the
*identical* set we still hit. So this is a **stable, structural** problem, not a new regression.
- **Body-slim mechanism (confirmed in the archive):** QIE-2511 learns body shape from **both** input
  images — the person **and** the garment references; product photos feature slimmer models, so their
  silhouette is **systematically** transferred onto the person (stronger the more slim-model refs). This
  is the same defect as the E-005 ~9–16 % proportion distortion, and it compounds with the PROMO/DensePose
  long-skirt distortion (DEEP_RESEARCH §12.1). Mitigations (garment-only crops — done; body-preservation
  prompt; **body-lock conditioning** — depth/pose ControlNet, §14.1b) are known; none fully closes it
  locally off-the-shelf → the body axis remains the standing kill-criterion risk (CONCEPT_REVIEW §5.4).
- **Colour + small-detail** improve with **resolution** (archive: blouse white→"light green" from 464→928
  px) and with **garment-only crops + no colour words** (H-COLOR, enforced in `prompt.py`). The board
  pixel-bottleneck (§12.4) is the remaining detail cap → high-res single-item repair is the answer.

**Net of §14:** the editing-first recommendation stands, but two project-internal facts must be acted on:
**unblock the editing spine from its own Phase-0 gate** (§14.1), and treat **body-shape preservation** as a
named, mechanistically-understood, still-open axis with a concrete lever to test (depth/pose conditioning),
not a mystery. Sources: `BUILD_PLAN.md` Phase 0, `ENGINE_CAPABILITY_PHASE0_2026-06-16.md`,
`archive/FINDINGS.md`.
