Autumn evening, theatre, neat social dress, so a day dress or refined knit with a skirt, a textured jacket, and neat flats or ankle boots. Cool evening, so an outer layer is warranted.

Jacket: Tailored textured blazer, slightly cropped, hip-skimming, charcoal grey (#3E4147), wool bouclé
Shoes: Ankle boots, low block heel, almond toe, black (#1C1C1E), smooth leather
Tights: Sheer sheer-matte tights, knee-covering, black (#202020), fine nylon
Bag: Small structured top-handle bag, black (#1C1C1E), smooth leather

Why this outfit works: The deep burgundy dress gives a rich autumn colour that looks polished under theatre lighting without being too dressy for a neat social evening. The textured charcoal blazer adds structure and depth, while the midi length and sleeves keep the look elegant and comfortable for sitting through a performance. Low block-heel ankle boots are easy to walk and sit in, and black tights and accessories tie everything together. The camel coat and soft stole add warmth for the walk to and from the theatre, and they come off easily in the cloakroom.
